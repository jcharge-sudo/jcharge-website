Services

Site survey today, support for the long run

We work with residential societies, businesses, and fleet operators, so the scope of work and equipment we recommend changes with the site.

Residential charger installation

Home charging points for private EVs, from consultation to commissioning, including wiring through your existing meter or a dedicated sub-connection.

Commercial & workplace charging

Charging bays for offices, malls, and hotels, sized to your parking layout, footfall, and available transformer capacity.

Fleet & depot charging

High-throughput DC charging for delivery fleets, cab operators, and bus depots, with load balancing across multiple bays.

Site survey & load assessment

Electrical load study, cable routing plan, and transformer capacity check, carried out before we quote any installation.

Approvals & subsidy support

Paperwork for state EV policy incentives and utility connection approvals, handled on your behalf with the local DISCOM.

Annual maintenance contracts

Scheduled servicing, firmware updates, and breakdown support once your charger is live, billed as a simple yearly contract.

Process

Installation, demystified

Five stages, start to finish. Most residential installs complete within two weeks of the survey; fleet sites take longer depending on transformer upgrades.

01

Site survey

An engineer checks load capacity, parking layout, and cable path in person.

02

Proposal & approvals

We size the charger, quote the cost, and file utility and subsidy paperwork.

03

Installation

Certified electricians install and wire the charger to your panel.

04

Commissioning

We test the unit, register it on the network, and hand over documentation.

05

Maintenance

Scheduled AMC visits keep the charger running and compliant.
